Ravi Stationery: Shop Policy (sample document)

Returns and exchanges
Unused items can be returned within 7 days if the bill is shown. Opened geometry boxes and used notebooks cannot be returned. Exchanges are allowed for the same price or higher.

Delivery
We deliver within 5 kilometres of the shop. Delivery is free for bills above 999 rupees. For smaller bills, the delivery charge is 30 rupees.

Discounts
Schools and tuition centres get a 10 percent discount on orders above 2000 rupees. During the festival week, all customers get 5 percent off on notebooks.

Opening hours
The shop is open from 9 am to 8 pm, Monday to Saturday. On Sundays it opens from 10 am to 2 pm.

Payments
We accept cash, UPI and bank transfer. Credit is given only to regular school customers and must be cleared within 30 days.
